Calvin Harris

Calvin Harris is a famous Scottish DJ, record producer, singer, and songwriter whose real name is Adam Richard Wiles. Early beginnings: He was born in Dumfries, Scotland, on January 17, 1984. He started making music as a teenager in his bedroom.Breakthrough: He gained early attention by uploading songs to MySpace. He released his first album, I Created Disco, in 2007.Global success: His third album, 18 Months (2012), broke UK records by having eight top-10 singles from one album.Major Hits and Collaborations He is known for massive electronic and pop hits like "We Found Love" (with Rihanna), "Summer", "This Is What You Came For", and "One Kiss" (with Dua Lipa).He frequently works with global pop stars, including Rihanna, Ellie Goulding, and Frank Ocean.He has won a Grammy Award and multiple Brit Awards. He has also been ranked as one of the highest-paid DJs in the world.
